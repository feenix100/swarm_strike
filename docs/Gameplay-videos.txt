SWARMSTRIKE 0.7 — OPEN FIELD GAMEPLAY

Three new 36-second videos, 1920x1080 at 30 fps, with stereo game-event audio.

01 Drone Assault: drone chase, side tracking, cockpit bursts and a rising finale.
02 On the Wing: close fly pursuit, reverse angles and banking orbits.
03 Crossfire: alternating teams, low passes, overhead combat and a wide pull-back.

All footage comes from native Rust/Bevy autonomous gameplay on the expanded map:
2,000-metre grid, 33 solid cover structures, 4 friendly craft and 4 opposing flies.
Enemy circuits use the full official MaleCNS annotated neuron graph. Offline
capture uses the identical neural_core.rs with lockstep 200 Hz neural updates and
60 Hz physics; rendering delays do not change simulated time. The director changes
cameras only. Hits, damage, bursts, cover collision and kills come from gameplay.
Sound effects are synchronized from actual game events, plus soft synthetic ambience.
No generated video, external music or staged hit/kill events are used.

All three files passed duration, frame-count, codec, audio and full decoding checks.
Per-file combat counts, metadata and checksums: Gameplay-video-validation.json.
